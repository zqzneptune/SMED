SMED Example Data: Havugimana et al. (2012)
-------------------------------------------

This directory contains protein elution profiles from the seminal study:
"A Census of Human Soluble Protein Complexes" (Havugimana et al., Cell 2012).

File Naming Conventions:
- [Instrument]_[CellFraction]_[Method].csv

Instruments:
- LTQ: Linear Trap Quadrupole mass spectrometer.
- Orbitrap: High-resolution Orbitrap mass spectrometer.

Cell Fractions:
- HeLaCE: HeLa Cytoplasmic Extract.
- HeLaNE: HeLa Nuclear Extract.
- 293NE: HEK293 Nuclear Extract.

Chromatography Methods:
- HCW: Hydrophobic Chromatography (Phenyl-Sepharose).
- TCS: Three-column System (Ion exchange, etc.).
- WAX: Weak Anion Exchange.
- SAF: Strong Anion Exchange.
- IEF: Isoelectric Focusing (pH ranges 3-10 or 5-8).
- SGF: Size Exclusion (Superose 6).

Reference Data:
- RefComplexes.txt: Semi-colon separated list of protein complexes for benchmarking.
